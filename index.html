Personal Life Dashboard

A private, single-user macOS desktop app for organising day-to-day life: goals, tasks, habits, journal, calendar and reminders, with an AI assistant that never acts without approval.

What it does

  • Shows today's priorities, calendar events, reminders, habits and goals in one place.
  • Reads Apple Calendar and Reminders on the Mac it runs on.
  • Optionally connects to the owner's Gmail account with read-only access, to suggest calendar events and reminders found in emails (for example appointments or bookings). Nothing is created without the owner's explicit approval, and the app never sends, deletes or modifies email.

Who it is for

This is a personal project used only by its developer. It is not offered to the public, has no accounts or sign-ups, and runs entirely on the owner's own Mac.
